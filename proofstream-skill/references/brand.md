# ProofStream brand and design guide

The source of truth is the current web brand material under `web/public/BRAND.md`, CSS tokens, logo files, and UI copy. The skill reuses that language rather than inventing a new protocol identity.

The current direction is dark-first with warm neutral surfaces, crisp borders, hard down-right shadows, and Departure Mono treatment for verifiable identifiers. `#00FF00` is reserved for released USDC and is not a generic success color, button color, or decoration. Use mono for addresses, hashes, transaction IDs, and state labels; use a readable proportional face for explanations. Keep copy short, declarative, and honest: “Awaiting confirmation” is better than “Success” when the chain has not settled.

Do not bundle a logo or font until the repository license and asset redistribution rights are confirmed. Do not use crypto-blue gradients, glassmorphism, floating particles, decorative motion, emoji UI copy, or invented trust badges. Status colors must explain a state and remain accessible in both light and dark modes.

For flyers, banners, social graphics, presentations, and motion, apply the production workflow and QA checklist in [creative-production.md](creative-production.md). The skill can direct an agent and validate a brief; it does not bundle a media generator or grant asset redistribution rights.
